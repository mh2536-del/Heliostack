Heliostack

A massing tool for an 80 × 80 m site. You draw where programs go and decide what matters. Heliostack tests thousands of variations, builds the tower that best balances your goals within your limits, and explains why every part of it exists.

How to work

  1. Draw program zonesPick a program in the bottom dock, then use Rectangle to drag a zone or Polygon to click its corners. Close a polygon by clicking its first point or pressing Enter.
  2. Tune each zoneWith Select, drag a zone to move it or drag its corner squares to reshape it. Its card sets the target floor area, the floors it can occupy and its priority where zones overlap.
  3. Set the limitsIn Parameters, choose floor-to-floor height, maximum height, site coverage, FAR limit and setback. These are hard rules the tower never breaks.
  4. Weight your goalsIn Generation, choose Solver (search for the best score) or Flock (every column follows local rules), then slide each priority up or down. The tower rebuilds only when you change a zone, a limit, a goal or the sun preset, then holds still.
  5. Read the resultEach goal shows its score. Hover any floor or patch of ground to see why it was kept, removed or held at its height.
  6. Compare iterationsHistory saves every rebuild with a thumbnail and full settings. Preview, restore, pin, overlay as a ghost, or compare two side by side.
